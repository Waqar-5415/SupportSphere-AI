# Usability test plan

**Participants:** 5–8 people, at least two non-technical. **Time:** 10–15 min each. Ask them to think aloud; don't help unless stuck > 1 minute (record the hint).

| # | Task | Success | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Create an account | Lands on the dashboard | 2 min |
| T2 | Find how to reset a password in the Help Centre | Opens the right article | 2 min |
| T3 | Ask the AI assistant why the app is slow | Reads an answer | 2 min |
| T4 | Rate the answer, then open a ticket from a "no match" reply | Ticket created with reference | 3 min |
| T5 | Find the ticket and say its status; read the staff reply | States status correctly | 2 min |
| T6 | Switch to dark theme and larger text | Setting saved | 2 min |

Record per task: completed (Y/N), time, wrong clicks, hints, quotes.

## System Usability Scale (1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree)
1. I would like to use this system frequently. 2. It was unnecessarily complex. 3. It was easy to use.
4. I would need technical support to use it. 5. Functions were well integrated. 6. Too much inconsistency.
7. Most people would learn it quickly. 8. It was cumbersome. 9. I felt confident using it. 10. I needed to learn a lot first.

**Score:** odd items (answer − 1), even items (5 − answer); sum × 2.5 → 0–100 (≥ 68 is above average).

**Open questions:** most confusing part? most liked? was it clear when the AI was working and when the ticket was received? what to change first?
